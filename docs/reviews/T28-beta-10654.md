# T28 MDE rollout — beta 10654

Date: 2026-09-30. T28 0.21. Promotion queue item 216.

## Why

Mihai: "for the defender findings, devices with vdi in the name should be excluded. named but excluded, because thats avd and out of scope".

The Defender findings are 🕳 Left out's 🔎 logon column (10648). A user with no Windows device of their own is often seen on AVD session hosts. Until now those hosts showed as "Defender only" or "in Intune — primary user …", as if they were devices the rollout could act on.

This is not a layout change, so there was no mockup.

## What changed

**The rule** (`MdeMembers.logonsFor`):

- A device whose name holds `VDI` in any case is AVD, out of scope. It is caught by its Defender name, or by its Intune record's name when Intune knows it.
- Whatever else is known of it, it reads: **⊘ AVD (VDI in the name) — out of scope, excluded**.
- It is sorted after the devices that count, however recent its logons.
- `isAvdName` is exported for the tests, and for any later use.

**On the screen:**

- The line is muted.
- A user seen only on VDI hosts says "only AVD — no device in scope".
- The 🕳 CSV carries the same words.

**Unchanged:** the KQL. It still finds AVD logons, so they can be named.

**Demo:** Sam also logs on to `cto-vdi-03`, his most recent logon.
