## One row per person

`MdeMembers.pilotsOf` now also returns `people` and `loose` (`pilotPeople`):

- **A person** is a pilot user, or the Intune primary user of a pilot device. The row lists every Windows device of theirs (by Intune primary user), in a pilot group or not. Each device has:
  - its pilot groups;
  - whether it is ⊘ excluded, has no Entra object, or is already in its country device group.
- **The country row** is the person's first non-pilot country: country, wave names, device group name, and whether the group exists and is nested.
- **State:**
  - `ready`: the country is known and has a device group name;
  - `none`: no country group, or a country with no device group name. They stay in the pilot, and the row says why.
- **Loose:** pilot members with no person to follow — a device with no primary user, not in Intune or not Windows, or a nested group. They are listed separately and never planned.

## The plan: ready for the wave

`MdeMembers.planPilotsReady(pm, keys, cfg)`:

1. **create** each missing country device group (assigned, the admin as owner, the usual description);
2. **add** each person's devices that aren't in their country group yet. A ⊘ excluded device is never added: it stays on the old set, and the plan warns about it;
3. **remove** each device from its device pilot group. Each removal carries `needsOk` pointing at its own country's add, so it runs only once that add is read back clean. A device already in its group waits for nothing;
4. **remove** the users from the user pilot groups.

`applyOps` gained `needsOk`: a step whose dependency didn't go through clean is skipped, with the reason on the ledger. So a device never leaves the pilot without landing in its country group.

The plan says what this means until the wave: they're ordinary members of their country, so the new policies stop reaching them and the old ones reach them again. When the country is nested, they move with everybody else. REMOVE is typed to apply. Undo from 📜 re-adds them to the pilot and takes the devices out of the country group; a device group the run created is left in place, empty.

## The view

- **Tiles:** people in the pilot, can be made ready, no country, and loose (only when there are any).
- **Rows:** each person row has its devices underneath, shown as "Person · devices | Pilot groups | Country → wave (nested?) | After the plan". "After the plan" reads, for example, "− out of the pilot", "+ into INT-SG-D-DEU (created first)", "already in INT-SG-D-NLD" or "⊘ kept on the old set — not added".
- **The bar:** "N people → out of the pilot · D devices into their country group · C device groups created", from the same plan function the dry run uses.
- **⚠ Before their waves go live:** the 10647 policy check is now a warning, not a block. It lists the policies that cover a pilot group but not the wave, to fix in ⚔️ or ⚡ before nesting the country.
- **Removed from 10647:** `pilotFresh`, `planPilotsOut` and the apply-time re-check.
