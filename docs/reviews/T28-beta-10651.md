## ⊘ 📋 A list

A switch at the top of the pane: **🔎 One at a time | 📋 A list**. Excluded now stays below both.

**What goes in** (`MdeExclude.parseList`):

- UPNs, e-mail addresses or device names: one per line, or separated by commas, semicolons or tabs.
- Quotes are trimmed. An Outlook "Name &lt;address&gt;" gives the address, and `mailto:` is dropped.
- A .csv or .txt can be picked (⭱ .csv / .txt) or dropped on the box. It fills the box, so the lines can be checked before the look-up.
- A CSV with a header row is narrowed to ONE column: the device name first, else the UPN, else the e-mail. An Intune device export also carries the primary user's UPN, and a device list is not meant to exclude its users.
- Lines are deduplicated, case-insensitively. Up to 500 per look-up; more are said, not dropped silently.

**Look them up** (`matchList`, `resolveList`):

| A line | Is matched as |
| --- | --- |
| with an `@` | a user, exactly, by UPN or e-mail: `userPrincipalName in (…) or mail in (…)`, 7 of each per request. Learn's known issues: an `in` filter takes 15 expressions by default. If that request fails, the Intune list still finds a primary user by UPN. |
| any other | a device, exactly, by name: the Intune Windows list first, then Entra (`displayName in (…)`, 15 per request). An Entra name whose device is in Intune is taken as the Intune device. |

- **Two Intune records with one name**, where only one synced in the last 30 days, is a re-enrolment. The recent one is taken and the others are said. If both are recent, the line says "2 devices have this name", with 🔎 open it in One at a time.
- **Each match is looked up** like a picked search hit, but lighter:
  - the direct groups only: the user's country group, and a device's country device group;
  - no "what reaches them".
  - A device from a list comes alone. Its primary user is named, not read or ticked.
- **Doubles:** a user's line claims their devices. A device that is also on its own line, or a user on two lines (UPN and e-mail), says "already in this list — with …".

## The table and the bar

**Columns:** Line → match, Now, Into the exclusion groups.

- **A user row** shows the name and country, then each Windows device underneath with its last sync, and stale or no-Entra-object where that applies.
- **Now:** excluded, "in INT-SG-D-NLD", or no country device group.
- **Into:** `+ PVM-UG-MDE-Exclusion`, `+ INT-SG-D-MDE-Exclusion`, `− out of INT-SG-D-XXX`.
- **Rows that can't be excluded say why:**
  - no user has this UPN or e-mail;
  - no device in Intune or Entra has this name;
  - several match;
  - not a Windows device;
  - could not be read.
- **Ticks** are each card's defaults: the user and their devices that synced in the last 30 days; a device alone. The header box ticks everything that can be ticked, or clears it.
- **The bar** reads "N users · M devices → the exclusion groups · K devices out of their country group", with the keep-on-the-old-set tick and ② Dry run.

## One plan

`MdeExclude.planAddMany` runs `planAdd` for each line and merges the steps per group:

- one add to the user exclusion group;
- one add to the device exclusion group;
- one removal per country device group.

A hundred lines are a handful of steps. Each step is written 20 members per request and read back.

- The plan opens under the list, above Excluded now. It is confirmed by a tick, since it only adds.
- The undo in 📜 is the exact inverse.
- After any run, the list's rows move with it (`patchList`). A list run ticks again whatever didn't go through.

## Also

- A device that synced minutes ago now says "last sync under an hour ago", not "0 h ago". This applies on the one-at-a-time card too.
