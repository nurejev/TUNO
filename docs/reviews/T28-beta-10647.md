## 🧪 Pilot members (👥 → 🧪 Pilots)

**The read.** The 👥 read also reads the direct members of the pilot groups in ⚙️ (`cfg.pilotGroups`): users, devices, and any nested group. A pilot group that isn't in the tenant is named.

**The standing** (`MdeMembers.pilotsOf`):

| Member | Its wave | ✓ in it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device | The device wave of its **Intune primary user's** country row | Its country device group holds it **and** is nested in the device wave |
| User | The user wave of its own country row | Its country group is nested in the user wave, or it is a direct member of the wave (a pilot's batches) |

- **⏳ Wave known, not in it yet.** The row says what's missing:
  - the wave or the device group doesn't exist yet;
  - the next 👥 Apply adds the device;
  - the country group isn't nested yet;
  - the pilot's batches haven't reached it.
- **✗ No wave:** no Intune primary user, no country group, not Windows or not in Intune, ⊘ kept on the old set, or a nested group (T28 takes members out one by one, never groups).

**⛔ The policy check** (`pilotBlocks`, "block that wave"). For each pilot group and wave pair, the wave is blocked when:

- a **new** policy includes the pilot group but not the wave (and isn't tenant-wide), or
- an **old** policy excludes the pilot group but not the wave or its twin.

Leaving the pilot would then lose a new policy or gain an old one. The members of that wave show ⛔ with the policy named, and the check line above the table lists every gap.

**The plan** (`planPilotsOut`):

- Only ✓ members have a tick box.
- The plan **only removes**: each member comes out of every pilot group it is directly in, with one step per pilot group and kind.
- Each member's wave is read **fresh** (`transitiveMemberOf`) before the plan is shown and **again right before the write**. A member no longer in its wave by then is left in the pilot, and the run says so.
- The REMOVE confirmation, the read-back, the 📜 run and the undo are the members engine's. `patchInput` moves the pilot lists both ways, with the names carried for the undo.

## A plan belongs to its pane

`clearPlan()` records the pane a plan is made on (`planPane`). `seatPlan()` hides the plan node on any other pane.

The plan isn't dropped: a half-read plan or a running ledger is back when you return to its pane. That is why it's hidden and not cleared.
